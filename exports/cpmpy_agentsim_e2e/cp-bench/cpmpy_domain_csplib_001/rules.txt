A set of cars is to be produced.
The cars are not identical because different options are available as variants on the basic model.
The assembly line has multiple stations.
Each station installs a specific option.
The cars must be arranged in a sequence such that the capacity of each station is never exceeded.
For instance, if a specific station can handle at most half of the cars passing along the line, the sequence must be built such that at most 1 car in any consecutive sequence of 2 cars requires that option.
The output must be the sequence of car types in the assembly line.
Each car type is represented by a number starting from 0.
