WHAT ARE YOUR
RIGHTS
You have the right to:
1) access your data—that is, to obtain confirmation from WARTA as to whether your personal data is being processed; to obtain a copy of it and information regarding, among
other things: the purposes of processing, categories of data, categories of data recipients, the planned retention period, and the source from which WARTA obtained it;
2) rectification of your data—that is, to correct or complete your personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
3) their erasure – in the cases specified in Article 17 of the General Data Protection Regulation, in particular where the personal data is no longer necessary for the purposes for
which it was collected, or where a valid objection has been raised against the processing of your personal data (provided that this does not interfere with the performance of the
contract or WARTA’s legal obligations);
4) restriction of processing—that is, the suspension of processing, in particular when you contest the accuracy of the personal data or object to its processing—for a period
allowing WARTA to verify the validity of the request;
5) data portability – that is, to receive from WARTA, in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, your personal data that you have provided to WARTA and that
WARTA processes based on your consent or on the basis of a contract concluded with you. In addition, you have the right to request that WARTA transfer your personal data to
another controller, provided that this is technically feasible;
6) to object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of your personal data based on the legitimate interests pursued by WARTA, including profiling based
on such interests, and to object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes by WARTA; Notwithstanding your objection, WARTA is entitled to continue
processing such personal data if it demonstrates the existence of compelling legitimate grounds for processing that override the interests, rights, and freedoms of the data
subject, or grounds for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims. You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct
marketing purposes by WARTA;
7) to lodge a complaint with the Supervisory Authority regarding personal data (which is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office).
If you have consented to the processing of your personal data, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of
processing carried out on the basis of consent prior to its withdrawal.
You have the right to request intervention from a WARTA employee regarding a decision based on automated processing, to express your own position, and to challenge that
decision by contacting WARTA through its agent, hotline, or by sending an email to:IOD@warta.pl.

WARTA collects data from external entities, i.e.: business information bureaus and credit
bureaus—to the extent necessary to assess insurance risk, provided that consent has
been granted in this regard; other insurance companies—to the extent of reported
incidents; in the case of business entities—from the National Business Register

reported claim.
WARTA collects data from external entities, i.e.: from the Insurance Guarantee Fund –
including regarding claims history and verification of the entity’s data; other insurance
companies – regarding reported incidents; medical facilities – regarding health
information, provided that consent has been given; assistance service providers –
regarding the course and results of services provided; in the case of businesses – from
the National Economy Entities Register (REGON) maintained by the Central Statistical
Office regarding data disclosed therein for the purpose of verifying its accuracy; and from
other entities – to the extent necessary for risk assessment, contract performance, or
evaluation of a reported claim.
